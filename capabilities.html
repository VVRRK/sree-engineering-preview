01 — EHV / TRANSMISSION
01 — EHV / Transmission

400 / 220 / 132 KV — The Bulk Power Layer.

The transmission layer moves bulk power from generation to load centres. Works at this level mean large steel, heavy equipment, long outages planned to the hour and zero tolerance for rework.

Our EHV scope includes substation bays and yards, EHV line sections, transformer and reactor bays, isolators, wave traps, CVTs and surge arresters — installed to transmission utility standards.

  • EHV substation bays & yards — structure, equipment, interconnection
  • EHV line sections — tower works and conductor systems
  • Transformer / reactor bays — complete installation scope
  • Transmission-class testing & commissioning support
02 — Sub-Transmission & Distribution

132 / 33 / 11 KV — The Network Layer.

This is the layer that delivers power to cities, industries and campuses — the networks that utilities, developers and plant owners live with every day. It's also where most of India's electrical capacity growth is happening.

Our scope at this layer spans 33/11 KV substations, RMU-based ring networks, HT feeders and interconnections, pole and small-tower lines, and the complete distribution equipment chain.

  • 33/11 KV substation works — the workhorse distribution voltage
  • HT feeder construction & extension — overhead and cabled
  • Ring main & RMU configurations for reliable network topologies
  • Distribution equipment scope — transformers, switchgear, protection

03 — HT & LT Distribution

HT & LT — The Point-of-Use Layer.

Below the network sits the infrastructure that facilities actually run on: the HT intake, the transformation to LT, and the distribution to every panel and load point. It's the most customised layer — every facility is different.

We handle the complete chain: intake installations, consumer substations, LT networks, panels and protection, and the metering interfaces that connect your facility to the utility.

  • HT intakes & consumer substations — your connection to the network
  • LT distribution networks — engineered per facility load
  • Panels, protection & metering — complete secondary scope
  • Upgrade & extension works — growth without rebuilds
04 — Substation Technology

GIS or AIS — a site decision.

The right answer depends on land, environment and lifecycle cost. Here's how they compare — and we execute both.

ConsiderationGIS — Gas-InsulatedAIS — Air-Insulated
How it worksLive parts sealed in SF6 gas inside metal-clad modulesLive parts insulated by air, in an open yard layout
FootprintRoughly 25–40% of an equivalent AIS yardLarger land requirement; classic open layout
Best suited forUrban sites, load centres, space-constrained plots, polluted or coastal environmentsGreenfield sites, transmission yards, cost-driven projects with land available
Environment & exposureSealed — largely immune to dust, moisture, pollution and wildlifeExposed to atmosphere; needs maintenance regime against pollution and weathering
InstallationFactory-built modules shipped to site; faster, cleaner erection; gas handling discipline requiredCivil + structural works, equipment erection, busbars and jumpers assembled on site
Maintenance accessEnclosed — scheduled intervention, condition-based monitoringOpen — everything visible and accessible for inspection and maintenance
Voltage classesCommon from 33 KV upward; standard at 132/220/400 KV in dense networksAll classes; dominant topology for 400 KV and greenfield EHV
Our executionBay erection, bus ducts, secondary wiring, gas & commissioning protocolsFoundations to energization — the complete yard build

Both technologies are fully within our scope. Tell us your site constraints and we'll recommend the right one — start the conversation.

06 — Testing & Commissioning

Verified before energized. Rooted in the field.

Testing & Commissioning

Every system proven before it's trusted.

Commissioning is where an installation either earns confidence or loses it. Our test scope covers insulation resistance and HV tests, transformer ratio/vector-group/insulation testing, protection relay secondary injection and functional schemes, earth resistance measurement, cable continuity and pressure testing, and complete pre-energization checklists.

  • Insulation & HV testing — cables, equipment, bus systems
  • Transformer testing — ratio, vector group, oil & insulation values
  • Protection scheme verification — secondary injection, trip logic
  • Earth resistance & pre-energization checks — measured, documented
