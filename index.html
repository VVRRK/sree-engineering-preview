06 — License & Credentials

Licensed. Compliant. Engineered for Responsibility.

The A-Grade electrical licence is the highest class of electrical contractor licensing issued by the state licensing authority — certifying competence to undertake electrical installation works without limitation on voltage class or contract value.

For clients, this means your electrical infrastructure is in the hands of a firm licensed and equipped to handle it at any scale.

Class A — Highest Grade
