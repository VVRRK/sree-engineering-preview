Our Story

A professional electrical contracting firm engineered for reliability.

SREE ENGINEERING is a Government Electrical A-Grade Licensed external electrical contracting firm focused on delivering dependable electrical infrastructure and power management solutions.

Led by a founder with deep, hands-on experience in the electrical contracting and infrastructure sector, the company brings practical field knowledge, technical understanding and execution-focused engineering to every assignment.

Our capabilities cover high-voltage, HT, LT and allied electrical works, with a strong emphasis on safety, quality, compliance, coordination and timely execution.
