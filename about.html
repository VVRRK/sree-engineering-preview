01 — Who We Are

An electrical practice with engineering at its core.

SREE ENGINEERING is an external electrical contracting firm based in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, executing projects for clients across India.

We exist to solve one problem well: delivering electrical infrastructure that is engineered and built to stay reliable. That covers GIS and AIS substations, 400 KV to 11 KV networks, transformer packages, HT/LT installations, underground cabling, panels, earthing and complete external electrification.

Our approach is deliberately simple: understand the requirement, engineer the right solution, execute with discipline, and hand over infrastructure that performs over the long term.
