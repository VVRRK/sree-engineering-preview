01 — Who We Are

A professional electrical contracting practice with engineering at its core.

SREE ENGINEERING is a Government Electrical A-Grade Licensed external electrical contracting firm based in Kukatpally, Hyderabad, serving clients across Telangana and India.

The firm is led by a founder with more than 23 years of hands-on experience in electrical engineering, contracting and infrastructure. This isn't a management-layer firm — it's an engineering practice where technical understanding, field knowledge and execution discipline come together.

We specialize in external electrical infrastructure: high-voltage and substation works, HT/LT installations, transformer erection, overhead line works, cable laying, panel installation, earthing and complete external electrification.

Our approach is simple: understand the requirement, engineer the right solution, execute with discipline, and deliver infrastructure that performs reliably over the long term.
